## 🎨 Style Guidelines

### Core Style Keywords
```
PC-98 style, retro adventure game scene, pixel art, 1990s anime style,
16-bit color palette, dithering patterns, scanline effect,
3:2 aspect ratio (960x640),
[Specific US city/state location and scene description],
diverse Americans of various ethnicities in authentic American urban setting,
wide angle establishing shot, full scene composition,
no signs, no text, no labels, no placards, absolutely no written words or letters
```

### Documentary Style Principles
- **Show, don't tell**: Use everyday scenes of ordinary people to reflect systemic issues
- **Specific US locations**: Real American cities and states (Ohio, California, Texas, etc.)
- **Authentic details**: SNAP applications, insulin prices, specific dollar amounts, real brands
- **Let the scene speak**: Objective presentation, let viewers feel the message themselves
